# Pivot only for visible hard technology

**Do not continue with the current “banking in iMessage” pitch unchanged.** It is an excellent execution path for S-Pankki’s partner prize, but the channel is a presentation flourish rather than a breakthrough: S-Pankki’s own brief already asks for conversational spending analysis and savings counterfactuals, and Junction’s 2020 overall winner already explored ML-driven financial-health analysis. The best risk-adjusted pivot is **Stoa’s construction-drawing challenge**, where the winning moment can be a question followed by a spatially highlighted, inspectable answer. The highest-ceiling—but materially riskier—choice is **Rokoko**, using live motion to teach a normalized skill that a simulator replays; a physical robot should remain an optional endpoint, not a dependency. Give Rokoko a strict **60–90 minute ingestion spike** and Stoa a short dataset/geometry spike tonight. If neither proves viable, keep S-Pankki but rebuild the product around a deterministic **financial decision twin** whose live state changes are visible beside iMessage. That sequence preserves a reliable submission while creating a credible path to the kind of visual hard-tech transformation that repeatedly reaches Junction’s overall stage.

## Finance satisfies the brief but hides its hard part

S-Pankki’s “Personal Economic Trainer” asks teams to use synthetic transaction data for a conversational experience that answers questions such as where money went and how reducing eating-out spend changes savings. **A chat interface, transaction categorization, proactive nudges and spending counterfactuals are therefore baseline compliance, not differentiation.** ([Junction announcement feed](https://se.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction)) The iMessage bridge is memorable for five seconds and makes onboarding feel unusually natural, but judges will still see text bubbles unless the product exposes an unmistakable computational artifact. Junction’s judging structure compounds that problem: challenge partners select their own winners, while five overall finalists emerge through face-to-face peer review before a live grand-jury pitch. A finance coach can win its partner room through fit and finish yet fail to cross the event because random peers cannot see why it was technically hard. ([Junction judging process](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#judging))

The historical comparison is uncomfortable but useful. Junction’s 2020 overall winner, Saavi Healthy Finance, used machine learning to analyze spending and subscriptions and explain financial health, then added a more theatrical AR interaction. ([Contemporary Junction 2020 coverage](https://medium.com/%40annaprist/hacking-conversational-ai-at-the-junction-2020-connected-hackathon-dbab972b51a)) More recent winners made their core capability visible: 2025’s WAVELET rendered a real-time GPU electromagnetic simulation, 2024’s BREAKDANCE paired a realistic inventory product with gamification and a memorable AI-rap pitch, and 2022’s winner turned a diary entry into emotionally legible generated art. ([WAVELET repository](https://github.com/ron0studios/WAVELET); [Junction profile of BREAKDANCE](https://www.hackjunction.com/blog/frank-sandqvist-story); [2022 winner account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kshgroup_we-won-at-junction-2022-activity-6995318216663216128-AoGW)) The recurring formula is **one-sentence comprehension, visible transformation, credible hard part, and a stage moment**.

Finance becomes defensible only when iMessage fronts a **living, auditable household model**. The demonstration should begin with “Can I afford an €18,000 car?”, resume automatically after read-only consent, calculate scenarios deterministically, expose the source transactions and assumptions behind every number, accept a correction, then fast-forward one week and trigger a plan-drift intervention. The audience should watch the emergency buffer and goal date move on a compact proof view while the messages arrive. This changes the thesis from “talk to your bank” to **“turn a vague life decision into a transparent plan that monitors itself.”** Keep finance only if that difference can be stated without saying *iMessage*, *LLM* or *open banking*.

## Stoa wins reliability while Rokoko wins ceiling

The current options separate cleanly into a reliable visual bet, a high-upside embodied bet, and several software fallbacks. Stoa asks AI to answer questions about real construction drawings with answers that are verifiable at a glance. That brief naturally produces a powerful 60-second sequence: ask which fire door serves a room, watch the system zoom to the relevant symbols and route, receive a page/grid/coordinate citation, then ask an ambiguous question and watch the system abstain. ([Junction announcement feed](https://se.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction)) **The 10× insight is not better PDF chat; it is that correctness becomes a spatial proof.** The team can narrow the weekend to one safety-critical question class, preserve geometry through the extraction pipeline and build a 15-case adversarial evaluation. This offers nearly hardware-level visual clarity without shared-equipment risk.

Rokoko has the greatest overall-winning upside. Its challenge supplies motion-capture equipment and mentoring for applications spanning animation, robotics and new interactions, while Rokoko’s own event framing emphasizes human motion for robot learning. ([Junction announcement feed](https://se.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction); [Rokoko event announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/Rokoko/comments/1xv921d/were_sponsoring_junction_hackathon_in_finland/)) The Device SDK provides direct TCP/IP pose access on macOS, Windows and Linux, avoiding a Unity dependency. ([Rokoko Device SDK](https://sdk.rokoko.com/)) The recommended concept, **ShadowTeach**, lets a person demonstrate one bounded movement, mirrors it on a browser skeleton, converts it into a normalized skill trajectory, rejects unsafe poses and replays it in simulation with latency and trajectory-error scores. If a robot slot is available, the same adapter can drive the arm; if not, the core demo still works. Merely mirroring an avatar is not enough—the transformation from human performance to reusable, evaluated skill is the project.

| Option | Showstopper moment | Reliability for two people | Overall ceiling | Decision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Stoa — Drawing Truth Lens** | Question becomes highlighted geometric evidence and an abstention when proof is insufficient | High, subject to drawing format | High | **Default pivot** |
| **Rokoko — ShadowTeach** | Live human motion becomes a scored, replayable robot/simulator skill | Medium-low unless recorded packets work first | **Highest** | Spike for 60–90 minutes |
| **Bahn Express — Recovery Dispatcher** | A delay cascade appears on a Europe map and one reassignment repairs cost/ETA impact | Medium-high if event data exists | High | Promote if the private brief confirms dispatch recovery |
| **Ignite × Intel — Exception Replay** | Camera sees a failed pick; a bounded human correction is replayed and verified | Low because arm, camera, calibration and safety all matter | Highest tier | Choose only with a working baseline and reserved access |
| **S-Pankki — Decision Twin** | A purchase changes buffers and goals, then real behavior creates a visible plan-drift intervention | **Highest** | Medium | Reliable fallback / partner-prize play |
| **Cala — Conflict-Aware Deal Graph** | A founder receives a sourced investor path with conflicts and warm-introduction edges exposed | High | Medium | Software fallback if data access is excellent |
| **Valio Aimo — Trusted Trade Agents** | A kitchen shortage triggers an explainable purchase and a deliberate human-escalation boundary | High | Medium | Strong partner fit; likely agent-heavy crowd |

Ignite × Intel can match Rokoko’s spectacle but has the worst dependency stack. Its public materials combine robot arms, cameras, Intel Core Ultra, OpenVINO and Physical AI Studio; OpenVINO’s documented runtime still requires correctly shaped observations, policy/action compatibility and robot/camera configuration. ([Intel × Ignite workshop](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#workshops); [OpenVINO Physical AI architecture](https://docs.openvino.ai/2026/physical-ai/explanation/architecture.html)) Do not attempt to train a general robotic policy during the weekend. If chosen, the viable scope is a pre-scripted pick with one visually detected exception and one bounded correction, protected by hard joint/speed limits. Rokoko is the better hardware bet because the input stream can be recorded and replayed independently of the physical endpoint.

## Tonight’s gates preserve upside without gambling Sunday

The preliminary submission is due before midnight Friday and the final submission at 10:00 Sunday, so equipment exploration cannot consume the first night. ([Junction schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule)) Use a short, explicit tournament rather than debating abstract concepts. The two brothers should split: one owns the ingestion/core technical spike, while the other collects the exact challenge cards, questions mentors, prepares fixtures and tests the visible demo path.

| Deadline from decision start | Test | Pass condition | Action on failure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **0–20 min** | Mentor/card check for Stoa, Rokoko, Bahn and S-Pankki | Exact required output, judging priority, usable data/SDK, number of teams, disqualifiers and equipment access are known | Remove any concept built on a guessed brief |
| **20–90 min** | Rokoko direct-ingestion spike using SDK packets or a supplied recording | Pose packets animate a browser skeleton with stable joints and timestamps; the same fixture replays deterministically | Stop Rokoko; do not wait for the suit |
| **Parallel 20–90 min** | Stoa drawing spike | One real drawing yields text/symbol geometry and a stable highlight tied to a question | Stop Stoa if evidence coordinates cannot survive extraction |
| **90–120 min** | One-sentence and 30-second demo test | A random hacker understands the before/after and can name the hard part | Choose the clearer concept, not the cleverer architecture |
| **By ~22:00** | End-to-end simulator/fixture loop | Complete visual story runs without shared hardware or live network dependence | Commit to Stoa or S-Pankki Decision Twin |
| **Before preliminary submission** | Mentor rejection test | Partner says the interpretation is on-brief and identifies no fatal judging mismatch | Re-scope immediately while the fallback remains intact |

The mentor questions should be blunt: “What would disqualify this?”, “How many teams are building chat with your bank?”, “Can we get a recorded mocap packet now?”, “Will coordinate-level evidence matter more than broad drawing coverage?”, and “What single outcome would make you choose us?” For hardware, secure a reservation only after the fixture loop works, and record a successful physical run as fallback. For every option, keep the 90-second pitch to a visible before/after, one technical sentence and one production implication.

## Conclusion

The finance work was not wasted: its consent-resume flow, deterministic calculations and messaging polish form the safest fallback. But the research changes its role from first choice to **insurance**. The first choice should become Stoa if coordinate-preserving evidence works; Rokoko should supersede it only when direct ingestion succeeds quickly enough that live equipment becomes an enhancement rather than the project’s foundation.

The correct pivot threshold is therefore not “which idea sounds cooler?” It is whether another track produces a **more visible hard transformation with an equally reliable fixture path tonight**. If that proof does not exist by the gate, ship the Decision Twin confidently and optimize for S-Pankki. If it does, pivot immediately—the Figma and iMessage work has already purchased the freedom to take that calculated risk.
