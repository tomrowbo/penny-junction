# 1. What tracks and specific partner challenges are now announced?

## Takeaway

The authoritative public picture is not a neat set of named tracks. Junction’s official site identifies **18 Challenge Partners**, while the public Junction Platform still exposes **no challenge cards** and even retains the stale line “Tracks are not locked yet.” The usable public inventory is therefore a mix of four well-described challenge briefs, several open-ended tool/hardware challenges, and several partner announcements with no public brief. Most challenges sit around AI, with physical AI/robotics in the Hardware Lab; “AI Infrastructure Track” is the only explicit track name found in a current official announcement. ([Junction 2026 site](https://2026.hackjunction.com/), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [Junction LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/))

# 2. What exactly must teams build, and what APIs, hardware and support are available?

## Takeaway

Teams can responsibly pre-plan around the build behaviors in the table above, but should treat them as announcement-level briefs. The full constraints, submission fields, datasets, eligibility rules and challenge rubrics were not public at 19:05 EET; the opening ceremony explicitly schedules “challenge videos,” and the public Platform exposes an empty challenge list before hacking begins. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026))

## Cited Findings

The build window starts at 19:30 Friday, a preliminary submission is due 23:59 Friday, submissions open at 12:00 Saturday and final submissions are due 10:00 Sunday. The official schedule places Google DeepMind, Verda and Arduino workshops on Friday evening, followed on Saturday by Cala, Intel × Ignite Next, Modal, Exa and other workshops. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule))

Teams are 1–4 people. Teams own their projects, but submissions are open-sourced under the MIT licence. ([Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [official FAQ](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#faq))

Challenge Partners select their own challenge/track winners using their own criteria. The overall Junction winner follows a different process: face-to-face peer review selects five projects, then those five pitch live to the grand jury. ([official FAQ](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#faq))

The official public prize pool is €100,000 across the weekend, but the site still says “Full prize breakdown coming soon.” The only numerical challenge/bounty prize found publicly is Verda’s €10,000 in platform credits; Atech promises prizes without amounts. ([official prize page](https://2026.hackjunction.com/), [Verda announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/), [Atech announcement in official feed](https://fi.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction))

The public grand-jury profiles provide useful overall-winning signals: Kia Kanninen wants a problem worth solving rather than an easy feature; Otto Söderlund wants a real user problem and something people return to; Hubertus van der Drift values speed, pragmatism and a unique insight that is 10× better/faster; Arturs Polis values hard problems with a path to production and scale; Daksh Raghuvanshi values explicit failure analysis and robust reward design. These are grand-jury preferences, not partner-challenge rubrics. ([official judge profiles](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#judges))

## Inferences

- Teams should not assume that using a sponsor’s API is mandatory unless the final challenge pack says so. Public wording makes tool use explicit for Cala, Context.dev, Exa, Aiven, Verda, Atech and the physical-AI partners, while several partner announcements only describe a domain. ([official Junction LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/), [official partner roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners))
- For hardware work, simulation before queueing for physical equipment is a supported strategy at least for Wendy-based deployment; the official Hardware Lab also provides mentors, but there is no public reservation/queue procedure. ([Wendy announcement](https://fi.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction), [Hardware Lab](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#special))
- A polished, narrow prototype with a defensible user need is more aligned with the published overall jury preferences than a broad demo that merely integrates many sponsor tools. This inference follows the stated problem, return-use, 10× insight and production-path preferences. ([official judge profiles](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#judges))

## Gaps

- No public source specifies challenge-by-challenge scoring weights, required APIs, mandatory datasets, submission evidence, IP exceptions, team eligibility, or prize amounts beyond Verda’s credit bounty. ([official FAQ and prize page](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#faq), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026))
- No public hardware inventory gives quantities, booking rules, per-team time limits, or whether robot/camera simulation assets are supplied for the Ignite × Intel or Rokoko challenges. ([Hardware Lab](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#special), [Ignite × Intel announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/), [Rokoko page](https://www.rokoko.com/mocap/junction-hackathon))
- The public S-Pankki announcement promises synthetic transaction data, but does not identify its schema, delivery mechanism or whether open-banking OAuth/API integration is part of the judged task. ([official S-Pankki announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/))

# 3. Which important details remain unknown, and what is the live decision implication?

## Takeaway

The largest uncertainty is not the partner roster; it is the missing challenge pack. At this timestamp, the opening challenge videos and participant-only/event-floor materials are likely the authoritative next source. Until then, any ranking of tracks must separate “confirmed partner” from “confirmed prompt” and avoid inventing requirements from a partner’s industry. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [official partner roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners))

## Cited Findings

The public Junction Platform page says tracks are “not locked yet,” shows no challenge section/cards, and only renders a short sponsor subset, while the newer official website and LinkedIn feed already show a much larger confirmed roster and multiple briefs. This makes the Platform’s public event description demonstrably stale/incomplete at 19:05 EET. ([Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [official partner roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners), [official Junction LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/))

The opening ceremony is scheduled for 18:00 and explicitly includes “challenge videos”; hacking starts at 19:30. Therefore, exact on-site briefs may be released in that 90-minute window even if public pages remain incomplete. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule))

The final ceremony schedule says challenge winners are announced at 15:10 Sunday and the overall Junction winner at 15:50, but does not publish the number or value of challenge prizes. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule))

## Inferences

- The best immediate decision process is to shortlist by known problem fit now, then re-score after watching the challenge videos on: exact deliverable, data readiness, mentor access, hardware contention, judging rubric and prize. This is an inference from the scheduled reveal and currently missing public challenge cards. ([official schedule](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#schedule), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026))
- S-Pankki is not yet a confirmed open-banking challenge: the confirmed public brief is conversational financial understanding over synthetic transactions. Open banking could be a product extension, but the announcement does not make bank connectivity a requirement. ([official S-Pankki announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/))
- The Hardware Lab’s breadth creates differentiation potential but also execution risk because public sources do not disclose equipment quantities or booking. A simulation-first architecture reduces that risk where supported. ([Hardware Lab](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#special), [Wendy announcement](https://fi.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction))

## Gaps

The following should be asked at the challenge booths or checked immediately when participant challenge cards appear:

- exact prompt and acceptance criteria for every Challenge Partner, especially Hostinger, City of Espoo, MariaDB, AfterQuery, Meta and Tieto;
- which challenges are mutually exclusive versus compatible with cross-cutting bounties such as Verda/Aiven/Atech;
- prize cash/credits/hardware per challenge and any special winning-team opportunity from Google DeepMind;
- provided datasets, API keys/credit limits, sandbox credentials, rate limits and offline fallbacks;
- hardware quantities, simulator assets, booking/queue rules and mentor hours;
- whether challenge data may be retained, demoed publicly and included in the required MIT-licensed repository;
- partner-specific judging weights and whether teams must use the partner technology or only solve the partner problem. The public sources do not answer these items. ([official FAQ and prize page](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#faq), [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026), [official Junction LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hackjunction/posts/))
